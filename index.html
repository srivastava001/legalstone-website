Who we are

Legal Stone LLP, previously practising as S&G Associates, is a team of advocates dedicated to providing legal services of a high standard. The firm is based at Shri Hari Tower, Mira Bai Marg, Narhi, Hazratganj, Lucknow.

The firm acts for individuals and businesses across civil litigation, criminal matters, taxation, consumer disputes, corporate and commercial disputes, and intellectual property rights.

The firm aims to work in a collaborative and inclusive environment, to keep pace with developments in the law, and to contribute positively to the legal community.

Frequently asked questions

General information about working with the firm.

How do I arrange a meeting with the firm?

Write to legalstone.inc@gmail.com, send a WhatsApp message to +91 96820 00160, or use the enquiry form on the Contact page. Meetings at the Hazratganj office are by appointment.

Does contacting the firm create an advocate and client relationship?

No. Sending an email, message or enquiry form does not by itself create an advocate and client relationship. That relationship begins only once the firm has confirmed in writing that it will act for you.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Bring any documents connected to the matter, such as notices, agreements, court papers, correspondence and identity proof. A short written timeline of events is also helpful.

Is the information I share kept confidential?

Yes. Communications with the firm are treated as confidential in line with the professional obligations of advocates. Please avoid sending sensitive details through the website form and share them at the meeting instead.

What areas of law does the firm handle?

Civil litigation, criminal matters, taxation, consumer disputes, corporate and commercial disputes, and intellectual property rights, along with related matters.

Where is the office located?

Shri Hari Tower, Mira Bai Marg, Narhi, Hazratganj, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

Privacy policy
